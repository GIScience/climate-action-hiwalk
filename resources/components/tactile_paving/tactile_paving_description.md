The map categorises paths based on their tactile paving.
Tactile paving increases the safety for people with visual impairments.
In contrast, missing or damaged tactile paving may make walking more unsafe and difficult for them.

Explanation of the different tactile paving categories from best to worst:
* Yes: Paths with tactile paving.
* Other signs: Paths without tactile paving but with other detectable features such as water drain or decorative tactile.
* Partial/ Incorrect: Paths where tactile paving is used but not in a sensible way, e.g. tactile paving on only one side of the crossing or tactile paving that leads nowhere.
* No: paths without tactile paving.
* Unknown: Paths for which there is no tactile paving information.

Data come from OpenStreetMap (OSM) and the categorisation is based on OSM tactile paving tags.
