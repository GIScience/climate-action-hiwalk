Pedestrian Crossings shows opportunities for pedestrians  to cross the road and classifies them by their amenities.

* **Traffic Signals**: Crossings controlled by a pedestrian traffic light.
* **Marked**: Crossings that are delineated by road markings.
May or may not have legal implications about the right of way
* **Unmarked**: Crossings that are implied by surrounding infrastructure, like e.g. kerb cuts.
* **Other**: Crossings tagged in OpenStreetMap (OSM) without further specification of their kind.

The categorisation is based on OSM tags `highway=crossing`, `crossing`, `crossing:signals`, `crossing:markings`, and `crossing_ref`.
