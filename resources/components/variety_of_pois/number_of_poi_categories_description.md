Number of Point of Interest (POI) categories present in each grid cell.

POIs from nine main categories representing essential facilities and services required for everyday life are included:
education, childcare, healthcare, everyday needs, public transport, green or natural spaces, culture and leisure, eating out, and pet care & services.

Facilities that are not expected to be within short walking distances from every neighborhood due to their centralized nature
(e.g., universities, large hospitals, airports) are not considered in the analysis (Mouratidis K, 2024)

### Description of POI categories
- Education: Schools (places where pupils are taught under the supervision of teachers).
- Childcare: Includes nurseries, kindergartens, and playgrounds.
- Healthcare: Includes doctors, clinics, dentists, and pharmacies.
- Everyday needs: Places for everyday errands and needs such as marketplaces, post offices, post boxes, cash dispensers, banks, grocery shops, convenience stores, bakeries, butchers, drugstores, hairdressers, and copy shops.
- Public transport: Stops with local public transport services such as bus, tram, and subway.
- Green or natural spaces: Public natural spaces such as gardens, parks, nature reserves, beaches, forests, and grasslands.
- Culture/leisure: Cultural places such as cinemas, libraries, community centers, theaters, and museums, as well as recreational facilities such as fitness centers, sports centers, and swimming pools.
- Eating out: Includes restaurants, food courts, cafes, pubs, and bars.
- Pet care & services: Includes services such as veterinaries and pet grooming, as well as places to walk your dog and dispensers for excrement bags.
